# 2099 (Apr 2024)

**Research status: Integrated (#213), research partial.** Upper Deck verifies a 100-card expansion and 1–5 player range. No card images or rules insert exist for this box (owner ruling, 2026-10-12), so Scheme Setup lines, Always Leads and part uses come from the owner's transcription of the cards (`OT`) cross-checked against C1's card text; both are cited in box data as `Card`. Wounds are used by Hulk 2099, both Villain Groups, both Masterminds, Befoul Earth Into a Polluted Wasteland and Subjugate Earth with Mega-Corporations.

## Catalog inventory

Upper Deck describes this as a **100-card expansion** for 1–5 players. C1 face metadata is indexed below; its ability prose is not rules evidence. The following group/title list remains a partial inventory:

- **Heroes (five):** Spider-Man 2099; Ghost Rider 2099; Doctor Doom 2099; Hulk 2099; Ravage 2099.
- **Villain Groups (two):** False Aesir of Alchemax; Alchemax Enforcers.
- **Masterminds (two):** Sinister Six 2099; Alchemax Executives.
- **Schemes (four):** Pull Reality Into Cyberspace; Become President of the United States; Subjugate Earth with Mega-Corporations; Befoul Earth into a Polluted Wasteland.

The C1 face index below records available printed titles, group/type, numeric values, and team/class/keyword metadata, with direct card-image URLs where supplied. C1 ability prose is not rules evidence. Group counts, fields absent from C1, and setup/component requirements remain unresolved.

## Rules and setup facts

Read from `OT`, cross-checked against C1 text (`Card`). Twists: Pull Reality Into Cyberspace 7, Become President of the United States 11, Subjugate Earth with Mega-Corporations 11 (extra Hero), Befoul Earth Into a Polluted Wasteland 8 (extra Hero). Alchemax Executives lead Alchemax Enforcers; Sinister Six 2099's card allows any "Alchemax" or "Sinister" group and is recorded as leading Alchemax Enforcers, which always meets it. Avatarr's Setup (extra Hero) applies only if that Tactic is on top after the Setup Adapt. Both Masterminds have six or four double-sided Tactics: the Epic side turns every Tactic over. There is no Henchman Group and no Bystander or Wound supplied by the box.

## Open questions

The official itemized contents list and any printed Scheme player limits; per-face copy counts of Villain and Mastermind cards beyond the C1 counts; whether Sinister Six 2099 may lead False Aesir of Alchemax (its name contains "Alchemax"); how the insert's rule against Epic Adapting Masterminds with Schemes that shuffle Tactics into decks of single-sided cards should be modelled; the Hero Names of the 2099 Heroes (recorded as the base character's, like Captain America 1941).
