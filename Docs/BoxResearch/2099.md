## Rules and setup facts

Read from `OT`, cross-checked against C1 text (`Card`). Twists: Pull Reality Into Cyberspace 7, Become President of the United States 11, Subjugate Earth with Mega-Corporations 11 (extra Hero), Befoul Earth Into a Polluted Wasteland 8 (extra Hero). Alchemax Executives lead Alchemax Enforcers; Sinister Six 2099's card allows any "Alchemax" or "Sinister" group and is recorded as an `alwaysLeads` choice of Alchemax Enforcers or False Aesir of Alchemax (#217), one drawn at random. Avatarr's Setup (extra Hero, then Adapt again) applies only if that Tactic is face up on top when the Tactics are first shuffled; the other three Alchemax Executives Tactics print no Setup, while every Sinister Six 2099 Tactic prints a Setup Adapt (C1, OT). Both Masterminds have six or four double-sided Tactics: the Epic side turns every Tactic over. There is no Henchman Group and no Bystander or Wound supplied by the box.

## Difficulty data (#269, 2026-10-14)

`Data/Difficulty/2099.json` (format v3, source `C1`, already in the box file's sources). Card effects were read from the C1 catalog's ability data (tags only are recorded here); the shared OCR file holds no faces for this box, so names follow C1.

- **Class conflicts.** None between C1 per-card classes and the box file's Hero class lists; C1 is used.
- **Copies.** C1 gives no Hero copy counts; the owner's LegendaryAssistant card data gives 5/5/3/1 for every 2099 Hero, which the file uses, so no copies are default. Villains use C1's counts. That data also agrees with C1 on every Hero card's class and cost. The Masterminds' Tactics are tagged by name, both faces.
- **Wants.** Cards with a Cyber-Mod or class Superpower ability want that class, at the most icons the card asks for in one ability (1 to 3); team Superpowers want the team (Spider Friends, Marvel Knights). Hulk 2099's Wound-counting cards want the Wounds stack (2 when they count Wounds, 1 for a single Wound). The Cyber-Mod keyword is not a want, since it reads the Victory Pile and not another card.
- **Draws.** Conditional draws are `draw 1`: Retractable Talons, Venomous Fangs, Cyber-Specter, Down in the Dregs.
- **Self costs.** Massive Gamma Detonation must discard two cards or gain a Wound (`discard`). Optional "may" effects (Undercover sends, Death Beyond Death's KO) are not costs.
- **Answers.** Push Pain Under the Surface and Cataclysmic Frenzy move Wounds out of hand or discard (`wounds 1`).
- **Masterminds.** Sinister Six 2099's Master Strikes make players discard (`hand-disruption 2`, `wounds 1`: most faces end in "or gain a Wound"); Goblin 2099 and Epic Goblin 2099 give every Villain in the city a Bystander on Fight (`bystander-pressure 1`). Alchemax Executives' Strikes push two HQ Heroes to the Escape Pile (`hq-disruption 2`) and the Cyber-Mod follow-up Wounds (`wounds 1`); John Herod KOs two of the fighter's Heroes on Fight (`deck-thinning 2`, 1 on his Epic face).
- **Villain Groups.** False Aesir: Heimdall 2099 (Ambush Bystander, Fight or Fail brings a Villain to the Bridge), Hela 2099 (Fight or Fail returns a Henchman; Escape Wounds), Loki 2099 (Master Strike shuffled back or played, `twist-density 1`) and Thor 2099 (discard or Wound on Ambush and Escape); their Uru-Enchanted Weapons are `attack-inflation` (1 per Weapon) on Fight. Alchemax Enforcers: Cyber-Nostra's conditional KO (`deck-thinning 1`), Whackoid and Venture capturing Bystanders on Ambush, Jigsaw 2099's KO, reentry and Escape Wound.
- **Schemes.** Pull Reality Into Cyberspace `attack-inflation 2`; Become President of the United States `hand-disruption 1` and `twist-density 1` (the discard-two counter replays another card); Subjugate Earth with Mega-Corporations `hq-disruption 2`, `hand-disruption 1`, `wounds 1`; Befoul Earth Into a Polluted Wasteland `hq-disruption 2` (the flush's Wound is optional). They amplify their loss condition's axis: `twist-density` for the first two, `hq-disruption` for the other two.

### Difficulty: no axis

- Hero Deck and Villain Deck order effects (Detect Vibrations, Fated Future, Overhorns and Underhorns's reveal), Tear Through Time Itself's extra turn and Subvert This New Future's send are untagged; so are static Attack changes on Villains and Tactics.
- The Hero Deck's top card going to the Escape Pile on Alchemax Enforcers' Ambush has no axis, so those Ambushes are untagged beyond the effects above.
- Tactics that only hit other players (Avatarr, Fearmaster, Sandwoman 2099) or only help the fighter (Electro 2099, Vulture 2099, Doctor Octopus 2099, Venom 2099, Tiger Wylde) are untagged.
