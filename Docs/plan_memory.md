# LegendaryPicker plan memory

**Snapshot date:** 2026-09-28
**Phase:** Design confirmed; implementation is tracked as GitHub issues under the "Legendary Picker v1" parent issue.

This is a handoff snapshot. `Docs/Plan.md` holds the decisions, the verified First Edition facts and their sources; `CONTEXT.md` is the domain glossary. Keep facts there, not here.

## Where things stand

- Repository: [RyanGano/LegendaryPicker](https://github.com/RyanGano/LegendaryPicker), branch `main`.
- `LegendaryPickerService` loads the core box catalog and setup rules from `Data/Boxes/core.json`, and serves `/api/health` (GET and HEAD, for the UptimeRobot keep-warm monitor, #31) and the rate-limited `GET /api/setup?players=1..5` (issue #4), which returns a `Setup/SetupGenerator` draw (issue #3). `LegendaryPickerApp` pings `/api/health` on load, lets the player pick a count (#5) and renders the draw as the Setup checklist, with counts, totals, cited Rule notes and per-line ticks that reset on a new draw (#6). Players choose which boxes to include (#8): the API takes `boxes=` and lists the boxes at `/api/boxes`, and with more than one box each rule note, glossary term and drawn card names its box. Display names are unique per kind across all boxes, enforced at load (#76). Base games are optional picks like any other box (#71, owner decision D1 in #34, 2026-09-29): a setup needs at least one base game and the core box is never mandatory. Every box file names its `ruleset` at the top level (`firstEdition`, or `villainous` for Legendary: Villains); several base games of one ruleset follow the first in catalog order. Heroic and Villainous boxes can be included together (#73): the Scheme and Mastermind decide the rules (owner decision D-mixed, #85, refined in #88, 2026-09-29): Villains rules when the Plot or Commander is Villainous, First Edition otherwise. Every other card is drawn from every included box whatever the pair, so nothing is locked out. A setup with cards of both rulesets is mixed: shared pools and all Bystanders together (VIL pp.20-21); under the Villains rules also every base game's recruit stacks and a choice of S.H.I.E.L.D. or HYDRA starting decks, while under the First Edition rules its Villainous cards bring only the parts they use (D-uses). The pair is drawn first, so every result is consistent; the result's `rulesReason` names the Plot or Commander that set the rules, or says there is none. The owner's #92 answers are recorded as source key D-readings in `Docs/Plan.md`. Box files are `schemaVersion` 7 (#106 added the required `about.released`, source key Q). Legendary: Villains is the second base game (#72, `Data/Boxes/villains.json`, source VIL, the Villains rulebook): 15 Allies, 7 Adversary Groups, 4 Backup Adversary groups, 4 Commanders and all 8 Plots. Cage Villains in Power-Suppressing Cells and Crown Thor King of Asgard (#84) set cards of a group beside the Plot through a Scheme's `setup.cardsBeside`, read from the Plots' card images; the cards fill no group slot (VIL p.17), and a drawn group puts only what is left of it in the Adversary Deck. Its setup table matches the core box's, with a 6th Ally at 5 players (`setup.extraHeroes`); its Solo (the rulebook's Advanced Solo Mode, Villains' only one-player mode) uses 5 Command Strikes and two play rules (`solo.playRules`, which replaced the core box's `twistKosHeroCostingAtMost`). Its stacks are Bindings, Madame HYDRA and New Recruits, stacks of their own; it follows the setup page's 12 Madame HYDRA and 41 Bystanders over the contents list's 15 and 42. A Villainous setup's notes and checklist use the Villains rulebook's words (Plot, Commander, Ally, Adversary Deck, …). The web app lists base games above expansions, ticks the core box on a first visit, remembers every ticked box in `localStorage`, and disables Generate with a hint when no base game is ticked. Dark City is the first real expansion (#40, `Data/Boxes/dark-city.json`, source DC): 17 Heroes, 6 Villain Groups, 2 Henchman Groups, 5 Masterminds, 11 Special Bystanders and all 8 Schemes. A Scheme can set how many cards of each Henchman Group go in the Villain Deck (`setup.henchmanCards`, #68), which overrides Solo's 3 under D2, so Organized Crime Wave puts all 10 Maggia Goons in a Solo game. Fantastic Four follows (#41, `Data/Boxes/fantastic-four.json`, source FF): 5 Heroes, 2 Villain Groups, 2 Masterminds and all 4 Schemes, each changing only its Twist count; it has no Henchman Groups. Paint the Town Red follows (#42, `Data/Boxes/paint-the-town-red.json`, source PTTR): 5 Heroes, 2 Villain Groups, 2 Masterminds and all 4 Schemes. Invade the Daily Bugle News HQ draws a Henchman Group outside the Villain Deck and puts 6 of its Henchmen in the Hero Deck (`setup.outsideHenchmen`, #77); a Scheme is dropped when no Henchman Group is left for it. Generator capabilities are still tested with made-up fixture expansions. Shared stacks (Bystanders, Wounds, Officers, and Sidekicks when a box has them) are summed over the included boxes' `components` (#35). Setup effects can add to counts as well as set them (extra Heroes, Villain Groups, Henchman Groups and Villain Deck Bystanders), per player count or only in Solo, and a Mastermind can carry them too (#36). Schemes can move cards between stacks and decks during setup (`setup.moves`: Hero cards, Henchmen, Bystanders, Wounds, Officers or Sidekicks into the Villain Deck, the Hero Deck, beside the Scheme or each starting deck), which replaced `heroCardsInVillainDeck` (#37) and moved box files to `schemaVersion` 3. Schemes can constrain the Hero Deck (required Heroes, at least or exactly N Heroes of a team or Hero Name, no two Heroes with the same Hero Name) and draw Heroes outside it into the Villain Deck, beside the Scheme or a stack set aside (`requiredHeroes`, `heroCounts`, `distinctHeroNames`, `outsideHeroes`); a Hero can carry a `heroName` (#38). Schemes and Masterminds can list setup steps that change no counts (`setup.steps`, each a short label in our own words with a source), which the checklist shows as lines to tick with a rule note each (#39). The docs now date the First Edition core box to November 2012 (#34 D6). Each setup also carries the glossary terms its components use: teams, classes and keywords with short original summaries and rulebook citations (#28); the UI for them is #29.
- GitHub Pages deploys the frontend from `main`: https://ryangano.github.io/LegendaryPicker/.
- The API is hosted on Azure App Service Free F1, Linux, West US 2, $0 a month (user decision, 2026-09-27). The web app is the one named by the `AZURE_WEBAPP_NAME` repository variable; the resource names and hostname are kept out of the repo. `.github/workflows/deploy-api.yml` deploys it from `main` through OIDC (the Entra app identified by the `AZURE_CLIENT_ID` repository variable, trusted for `refs/heads/main` only); the Pages build points at it through the `VITE_API_BASE_URL` repository variable (#7). Any other Azure resource needs explicit authorization.

## Resolved in the 2026-09-27 session

- The whole core-box catalog is confirmed. An earlier note wrongly listed Sentinel and Hand Ninjas as Villain Groups; they are Henchman Groups.
- Solo uses 1 Master Strike, not 5.
- A Scheme's setup overrides the Solo setup (designer ruling for *Secret Invasion*; the user accepted applying it to Bank Robbery, Killbots and Legacy Virus).
- Always Leads groups and Scheme-required groups fill slots rather than adding groups; the user accepted the extension to Skrulls.
- Generation is a table draw (Scheme, Mastermind, required groups, remaining groups, Heroes), replacing the earlier "uniform over distinct setups" wording.
- Unsourced conflicts fall back to the base Legendary rules; expansion rules apply only when that box is included.
- The planning "cases" are GitHub issues; each issue lists the fixed-result test cases it must pass.
- Owner decision D-heroic (2026-10-04, #110): Fear Itself works without Legendary: Villains. With a Heroic base game and Fear Itself but no Villainous base game, every pair follows the First Edition rules, and Fear Itself's `otherRuleset.standIns` (FI p.2) replace parts no included box supplies: Wounds for Bindings (the player may use Bindings), S.H.I.E.L.D. Officers for Madame HYDRA, and +1 Recruit for a New Recruit gain.
- Owner decision D-shards (2026-10-04, #107): the Shard tokens are double-sided, so Guardians of the Galaxy's 18 tokens cover Unite the Shards' 30 Shards. A setup lays out all the Shard tokens from the included boxes, with no stand-ins, and never drops the Scheme for lack of Shards.
- `/api/boxes` lists base games first, then by release month (`about.released`, #106); the draw still uses catalog (file) order.
- Secret Wars Volume 1 (#113) is integrated with its Sidekick stack. Schemes can now draw other Masterminds (`setup.outsideMasterminds`): Dark Alliance sets one aside, Master of Tyrants shuffles 4 Tactics each of 3 into the Villain Deck. Build an Army of Annihilation (#116) puts 10 Henchmen of any one Henchman Group outside the Villain Deck in the KO pile (owner decision D-ko-henchmen, 2026-10-04). A set-aside Mastermind's `joins` says when it comes into play (#122).
- Secret Wars Volume 2 (#117) is integrated: 16 Heroes, 6 Villain Groups, 3 Henchman Groups, 4 Masterminds and 8 Schemes, read from the C1-linked card faces (`Card`). Sinister Ambitions (#119) moves 10 Ambition cards, a part brought in by use, into the Villain Deck (D4 excludes only the Ambition play mode). Dark City's Transform Citizens into Demons takes any Jean Grey (owner decision D-jean-grey, #122). Secret Wars sets 3 other Masterminds aside (its Twists 1-3 add one each); The Mark of Khonshu requires Khonshu Guardians and adds one outside Hero to the Villain Deck. The box reuses Secret Wars Volume 1, Paint the Town Red and Dark City terms.
- Captain America 75th Anniversary (#121) is integrated in full: 5 Heroes, 2 Villain Groups, 2 Masterminds and 4 Schemes, read from the C1-linked card faces (`Card`). Brainwash the Military moves 12 Officers into the Villain Deck. Its Heroes keep their printed Hero Names (Captain America (Falcon), Captain America 1941, Steve Rogers, Director of S.H.I.E.L.D.), so none shares the core Captain America's.
- Deadpool (#128) is integrated in full: 5 Heroes, 2 Villain Groups, 2 Masterminds and 4 Schemes, read from the C1-linked card faces (`Card`). Its Deadpool Hero is displayed as Deadpool (Mercs for Money) (Hero Name Deadpool); Schemes that need Deadpool accept either Deadpool Hero. Its Heroes carry the new Mercs for Money and HYDRA teams.
- Noir (#130) is integrated in full: 5 Heroes, 2 Villain Groups, 2 Masterminds and 4 Schemes, read from C1 and its card-image links (`Card`). Hidden Heart of Darkness shuffles the drawn Mastermind's own Tactics into the Villain Deck (`setup.ownTactics`). Detective Wolverine is a Bystander, so it is not a Scheme. Heroes keep their printed Hero Names.
- X-Men (#132) is integrated in full: 15 Heroes, 7 Villain Groups, 5 Henchman Groups, 6 Masterminds and 8 Schemes, read from C1 and its card-image links (`Card`). New optional fields: a Scheme's `setup.wounds` (Anti-Mutant Hatred's 30-Wound stack), a required group's `cards` (all 10 Brood or Sentinels as an extra Henchman Group, even in Solo) and a Mastermind's `alsoLeads` (Deathbird's Shi'ar Henchman Group). Horrors are a new part laid out only for Horror of Horrors; each Mastermind's Epic side is a setup step, not drawn, and is recorded as `epic` (printed title, extra parts used) on every Epic Mastermind in X-Men, Champions and Spider-Man Homecoming (#151). The Dark Phoenix Saga takes either Jean Grey (D-jean-grey). Beast is "Beast (X-Men)".
- World War Hulk (#143) is integrated in full: 15 Heroes, 7 Villain Groups, 3 Henchman Groups, 6 Transforming Masterminds and 8 Schemes, read from C1 and its card-image links (`Card`). New optional field `heroNameContains` on a Hero count or outside Hero draw (Fall of the Hulks: exactly 2 Heroes with "Hulk" in their Hero Names; Mutating Gamma Rays and Shoot Hulk into Space: an extra Hulk set aside). Cytoplasm Spike Invasion sets all 10 Spikes and 20 Bystanders beside it; World War Hulk sets 3 Lurking Masterminds aside. Transformed cards stay out of the Hero Deck. Owner question: whether Wounded Fury alone should lay out the Wound Stack (read literally: it does not).
- Champions (#136) is integrated in full: 5 Heroes, 2 Villain Groups, 2 Masterminds and 4 Schemes, read from C1 and its card-image links (`Card`), with no new capability. Clash of the Monsters Unleashed sets the 8 Monsters Unleashed Villains beside it through `cardsBeside` (so Fin Fang Foom is never drawn with it) and 6 Wounds per player; Divide and Conquer sets 7 Heroes. Totally Awesome Hulk, Fin Fang Foom and Pagliacci use Wounds. Owner question: whether the Monster Pit group also fills a Villain Group slot (read literally: it does not).
- Spider-Man Homecoming (#134) is integrated in full: 5 Heroes, 2 Villain Groups, 2 Masterminds and 4 Schemes, read from C1 and its card-image links (`Card`), with one new optional field. Explosion at the Washington Monument sets aside 18 Bystanders and 14 Wounds; Scavenge Alien Weaponry adds an extra Henchman Group of 10 cards, even in Solo, through the new Scheme field `setup.extraHenchmanCards`. Wall-Crawl reuses Paint the Town Red's term.
- Civil War (#125) is integrated in full: 16 Heroes, 7 Villain Groups, 2 Henchman Groups, 5 Masterminds and 8 Schemes, read from the C1-linked card faces and C2 (`Card`). Its 15 Sidekicks, 15 Grievous Wounds and 7 Bystanders join the shared stacks; its Aspiring Hero Bystanders gain Sidekicks, so `bystanderUses` lays the Sidekick stack out whenever the box is included. A Divided Card counts as one card and takes its left half's Hero Name and team (Storm & Black Panther: Storm, X-Men). Avengers vs. X-Men uses the new `teamSplit` Hero rule (3 Heroes of one team, 3 of another).

- Owner decision D-scheme-first (2026-10-05, #138): the Scheme is drawn first from every included Scheme its card allows at the player count, then a Mastermind it doesn't exclude (`excludesMasterminds`, box data checked by a test that derives it from the cards). The per-draw completions check is gone; the loader requires every requirement to be in its own box unless `otherBox` allows another box. Without the core box, the Kree-Skrull War still uses the core Skrulls (labelled "required by the Scheme, from" the core box), The Dark Phoenix Saga takes Dark City's Jean Grey, and Super Sentinels uses any included Henchman Group. The service test suite dropped from about 15-22 minutes to about 30 seconds.

- Owner direction (2026-10-05, #154): box tests stay lean. Each box gets a load-and-counts row in `BoxDataTests` plus 1 to 3 tests for what is unique to it; generator capabilities are tested once with the made-up fixtures (see CLAUDE.md Tests).
- Owner answer on #141, built in #150: a Scheme's specific required card (a Hero with a given Hero Name, a named group) comes from the ticked boxes whenever one matches; only otherwise from an unticked box, labelled. Basic parts keep coming from the first loaded box that supplies them.
- Owner decision D-hero-identity (2026-10-05, #149, from #141): a two-team Divided Card Hero counts as either team (one at a time), a two-name Hero under either name, and a version under its character's Hero Name (Spider-Man Noir is Spider-Man). Recorded in `Docs/Plan.md`.

- Marvel Studios Phase 1 (#147) is integrated as the second First Edition base game: 7 Heroes, 5 Villain Groups, 4 Henchman Groups, 3 Masterminds and 8 Schemes from C1 and its card-image links (`Card`), rules from P1 (PDF pages). Its printings of core cards (all 7 Heroes, Loki, Red Skull, Enemies of Asgard, HYDRA, Super Hero Civil War, Cosmic Cube) are the core cards under #34 D5, applied here: the box lists them in `reprints`, each is in the pool once when both base games are ticked, drawn as usual with Phase 1 alone, and a drawn reprint names every ticked box that holds it. Their setup data matched the core cards, so none is a separate version. Setup numbers win over the contents list (41 Bystanders, 5 Master Strikes). When the included Heroes can't meet a Scheme's Hero rules (Avengers vs. X-Men with Phase 1 and Civil War), the missing Heroes come from other loaded boxes of its ruleset, named with their box. Owner rulings 2026-10-05: the checklist only adds, never says what to leave out; versions of one character share its Hero Name.
- Ant-Man (#163) is integrated in full: 5 Heroes, 2 Villain Groups, 2 Masterminds with Epic sides and 4 Schemes, read from C1 and its card-image links (`Card`). Giant Ants lists its Twists (players plus 6) per player count; Trap Heroes in the Microverse puts an extra Hero in the Villain Deck; Age of Ultron adds a Hero at 4-5 players. Morgan Le Fay and both Villain Groups use Wounds.
- Revelations (#170) is integrated in full: 9 Heroes, 4 Villain Groups, 2 Henchman Groups, 3 Masterminds with Epic sides and 4 Transforming Schemes, read from C1 and OCR of its card-image links (`Card`). House of M asks for 4 Heroes of one team (`teamSplit` `[4]`, now allowed with one team, since the card lets any team replace its X-Men) and a Scarlet Witch in the Villain Deck; Secret HYDRA Corruption's Twists depend on the player count and it uses Officers. Every Villain Group and Mastermind uses Wounds, as does The Korvac Saga.
- Venom (#165) is integrated in full: 5 Heroes, 2 Villain Groups, 2 Masterminds with Epic sides and 4 Schemes, read from C1 and its card-image links (`Card`). Maximum Carnage sets 6 Wounds per player; Symbiotic Absorption sets one other Mastermind aside and adds its Always Leads group as an extra Villain Group (`bringsAlwaysLeads`, drawn from Masterminds leading a different group). Hybrid, Poison Thanos and Life Foundation use Wounds. The Hero Venom is "Venom (Venomverse)" beside the Villains box's Hero Venom.
